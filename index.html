How it works

1. Tap Find my location and allow location access.

2. The map draws a 100-metre circle around you.

3. Nearby places are grouped into Food, Beer, Laundry, Attractions and Other. The list and map show places found within 100 metres.

The attraction data comes from OpenStreetMap contributors. Results depend on what has been mapped nearby. Location access generally requires HTTPS or localhost.
